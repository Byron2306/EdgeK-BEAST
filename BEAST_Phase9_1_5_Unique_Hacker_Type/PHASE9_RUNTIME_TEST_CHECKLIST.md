# Phase 9 Runtime Acceptance Checklist

1. Start BEAST through the Electron launcher, not `file://`, then fully restart Electron.
2. Check 1920×1080 at 100%, 1600×900, 1366×768, and 125%/150% zoom.
3. Verify the top chrome header does not clip mission text or connection controls.
4. Navigate every route and confirm each page header receives the correct premium PNG icon.
5. Confirm matrix rain, moving grid and page scan are visible but do not reduce text contrast.
6. Confirm Core Health and telemetry cards show moving heartbeat strips.
7. Confirm Studio System Topology shows the live dotted world visualizer and pulsing nodes.
8. Confirm reduced-motion disables sweeps and atmosphere correctly.
9. Verify there is one page outlet child, no document horizontal overflow and no duplicate IDs.
10. Exercise Settings atmosphere and motion controls.
