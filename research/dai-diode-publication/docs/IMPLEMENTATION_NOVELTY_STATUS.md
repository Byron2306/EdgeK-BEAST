# DAI-Diode implementation-topology novelty review

**Status:** active source-backed investigation  
**Priority gate:** withheld  
**Purpose:** test the physical and architectural mechanism chain, not only the abstract functional feature list

## Why this layer is necessary

The DAI-Diode novelty question cannot be reduced to whether deterministic logic, provenance, post-quantum cryptography, eBPF, attestation, or quorum existed independently. Each did.

The material question is whether a prior public and reproducible system joined them into the same epistemic and authority topology:

```text
Sophia acquisition and source support
  -> Seraph/Metatron adversarial challenge, deception, cadence, harmonic and consequence analysis
  -> BEAST deterministic typing, composition, promotion and authority compilation
  -> sealed proof-carrying Crystal custody and Crystal Bus transfer
  -> Sensorium/eBPF/XDP current-world observation and explicit loss evidence
  -> ML-KEM-bound heterogeneous Commons validation
  -> Arda appraisal and physically attested execution
  -> postcondition evidence
  -X-> no direct escalation into production authority
```

## Additional dimensions now frozen

### ML-KEM is not merely encryption

DAI-Diode uses ML-KEM-768 for pairwise Commons session establishment and confirmation. The evidence records three confirmed nodes, six pairwise transcript edges, public-key/ciphertext/confirmation digests, and a policy that shared-secret bytes are never serialized.

The novelty target is not “first use of ML-KEM.” It is the binding of post-quantum session evidence into the exact semantic quorum proposition, participant identity, evidence root, attestation lane, freshness window, and authority ceiling.

### Cadence is epistemic time governance

Cadence is not a cron schedule. It governs when observations must recur, how long corroboration remains valid, when leases and capabilities decay, how frequently adversarial challenge must be refreshed, and when missed timing becomes evidence of uncertainty or invalid applicability.

The novelty target is temporal state becoming a signed input to what the system may believe, say, promote, or execute.

### Harmonic engine is behavioral coherence analysis

The Seraph/Metatron harmonic engine evaluates timing intervals, jitter, drift, burstiness, entropy, sequence tempo, scope-specific baselines, resonance, discord, and confidence.

It must be classified as a behavioral and temporal consistency organ, not as a generic scheduler. Its role is to detect whether independent observations and organ behavior cohere with the declared world state and historical cadence.

### Deception is a permanent immune organ

Seraph deception is not a one-off penetration test. Controlled false routes, stale identities, malformed evidence, contradictory state, capability bait, adversarial substitutions, and mutation variants are part of the promotion and continued-trust process.

A candidate capability should not gain or retain reusable deterministic authority merely because it succeeds on ordinary inputs. It must survive governed deception and fail with the correct refusal or rejection reason.

### World state is the applicability root

World state binds topology, policy generation, clock source, lease freshness, boot identity, runtime identity, resource and pressure state, network scope, observed negative conditions, and current evidence into the exact applicability decision.

A stored crystal may remain byte-valid while becoming inapplicable because the world has changed. This separates durable semantic knowledge from temporary permission to speak or act.

## Current strongest implementation-level candidate claim

> **To our knowledge, DAI-Diode is the first publicly reproducible deterministic intelligence architecture to promote learned concepts into sealed proof-carrying capabilities; transport them through custody-governed descriptor transfer; bind their semantic applicability to explicit kernel-observed world state and observation-loss evidence; establish post-quantum Commons lanes with ML-KEM; subject promotion and continued trust to cadence, harmonic-coherence, deception and role-diverse quorum; and admit physically attested execution without allowing successful execution evidence to directly escalate production authority.**

This is a candidate claim only. It remains withheld until:

1. every internal mechanism is bound into the final immutable lineage;
2. ablation proves each named organ is causally necessary for its corresponding claim;
3. mutation proves the declared non-escalation and fail-closed boundaries;
4. clean-room reproduction confirms the same normalized semantic and authority result;
5. the external search finds no single full-stack predecessor;
6. an independent reviewer challenges every asserted absence.

## Claim discipline

None of the following is novel by itself:

- ML-KEM;
- memfd sealing;
- SCM_RIGHTS descriptor transfer;
- eBPF or XDP;
- cadence or leases;
- time-series anomaly detection;
- deception or mutation testing;
- world-state models;
- separation of duties;
- multi-agent workflows.

The potentially novel object is the **complete mechanism chain and its enforced authority invariant**.
