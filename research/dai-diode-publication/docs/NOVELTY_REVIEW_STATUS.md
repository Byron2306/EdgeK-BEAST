# DAI-Diode novelty review status

**Status:** source-backed first pass in progress  
**World-first gate:** withheld  
**Technical-validity gate:** evaluated separately from historical priority

## Current conclusion

The first primary-source pass has not identified a single prior system matching the full DAI-Diode conjunction.

It has identified three genuinely dangerous precursor clusters:

1. **Distributed authorization and logical trust** — KeyNote, Binder, Delegation Logic, SecPAL, DKAL/DKAL*, and SAFE already establish that deterministic logic can operate over credentials, delegation, authenticated statements, linked contexts, multiple principals, and federated domains.
2. **Confidential multiparty governance and transparent receipts** — CCF and SCITT already establish hardware-backed integrity, auditable ledgers, signed statements, offline-verifiable receipts, replicated state, and multiparty governance.
3. **Semantic validator quorum for agentic infrastructure** — Semantic Quorum Assurance already establishes declarative execution contracts, cryptographic evidence chains, diverse validator panels, risk-adaptive quorum, vetoes, and a separate sovereign execution gate.

These systems defeat broad claims such as:

- first deterministic trust reasoning;
- first signed provenance architecture;
- first distributed authorization logic;
- first attested consortium governance;
- first semantic quorum;
- first proof-carrying policy enforcement.

They do **not**, on the evidence reviewed so far, establish the entire DAI-Diode architecture in one publicly reproducible system.

## Strong candidate claim under review

The strongest precise candidate is:

> **To our knowledge, DAI-Diode is the first publicly reproducible intelligence operating architecture in which independently learned concepts gain reusable deterministic authority only after transfer testing, adversarial challenge, typed semantic verification, organ-bound evidence, current-world-state binding, attested role-diverse quorum, and physically attested execution, while execution evidence is explicitly barred from directly modifying production authority.**

This wording is deliberately narrower than “first deterministic intelligence.” Each mechanism in the sentence must be:

- defined in machine-readable terms;
- evidenced in the final capsule;
- represented in the prior-art claim chart;
- independently reproduced;
- absent from every alleged full-stack predecessor.

## Why the candidate may survive

### Against logical trust systems

SAFE, SecPAL, DKAL, Binder, KeyNote, and Delegation Logic cover much of the deterministic trust substrate. They do not presently appear to include the complete combination of:

- independently learned concept promotion;
- transfer testing before reusable authority;
- typed semantic verification of new compositions;
- organ-bound evidence;
- current-world-state binding;
- physically attested execution;
- role-diverse attested quorum over one exact proposition;
- shared text and visual expression closure;
- an invariant preventing execution evidence from escalating production authority.

### Against CCF and SCITT

CCF and SCITT are the closest provenance, attestation, receipt, and governance precursors. They do not themselves define an intelligence operating architecture that promotes learned concepts into reusable deterministic semantic capabilities and composes them into bounded answers/refusals with one-semantic-object text and visual expression.

### Against Semantic Quorum Assurance

SQA is the closest terminology and validator-governance precursor. It is explicitly designed for **non-deterministic** validator agents. DAI-Diode’s claimed novelty is not the word “semantic quorum”; it is the use of an attested, role-diverse quorum to certify a deterministic, provenance-closed semantic derivation and authority-bounded expression, with execution still unable to grant production authority.

## Matrix conventions

The CSV uses:

- `PRESENT` — explicitly supported by the cited primary source;
- `PARTIAL` — adjacent or configurable, but not the same mechanism or not intrinsic to the system;
- `ABSENT` — not part of the documented architecture reviewed;
- `UNKNOWN` — source review is incomplete;
- `PENDING` — DAI-Diode target feature awaits final independent evidence.

Absence is always provisional: it means “not found in the reviewed primary source,” not proof that no implementation or later paper contains the feature.

## Stop rule

A prior work defeats the candidate claim only if one publicly documented system contains the complete claimed mechanism chain—not merely one or several components.

If a full-stack predecessor is found, the technical BDI result remains valid. The priority claim must then narrow to the first independently reproduced implementation, first specific authority invariant, first specific attested composition protocol, or another mechanism-level distinction supported by the evidence.
